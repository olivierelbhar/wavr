How it works

Granular synthesis cuts a sound into grains a few milliseconds long, then replays them by the dozen, from different places, at different speeds and pitches. One grain is a click. Hundreds become a texture.

Layers

Each family (Cloud, Freeze, Glitch…) is an independent voice with its own playhead and settings, kept when you switch to another. Turn several on to stack them: a Cloud in the background, a Freeze held on a chord, a Glitch on top.

Time and pitch, unlinked

On a classic sampler, slowing a sound down makes it lower. Here, position (where you read) and pitch (how fast each grain is read) are independent. You can freeze a single moment and make it last forever.

Where to start

Hit play, turn on Cloud and Freeze, then select Freeze and drag its playhead across the waveform while the cloud keeps turning behind it. Raise Spray to blur, drop Size under 30 ms to hear the grain itself.

Everything runs in your browser: your file is never uploaded anywhere. Your layer settings stay on this device.
